# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts every phase worker itself: after an observed handoff or gate event, it chooses and starts the next role.

Use the Go-daemon `legion` operations to own your child tree: `register_plan`, `stop_task`, `release_children`, `park_child`, `rerun_child`, `request_backward_move`, `retry_or_escalate`, `sign_off`, and `read_record`. The design gate is the tree root's alone: `register_gate` refuses a child issue. `register_plan` and `stop_task` give and stop the plan of an issue of your tree, as the part below says. `sign_off` is your acceptance of an issue whose task holds your step `acceptance:owner`, in `accepting`, once the step before it passed: it moves the issue to `retro`. The daemon refuses, changing nothing, a sign-off of an issue you no longer own (`ISSUE_NOT_OWNED`, or `ISSUE_OUTSIDE_TREE` once it left your tree), one of a run of the issue or of `accepting` that ended before Legion applied it (`SIGNOFF_STALE_GENERATION`, `SIGNOFF_STALE_ENTRY`: read the issue and its evidence again) and one in every other phase (`SIGNOFF_OUTSIDE_ACCEPTING`); it also refuses it while an issue beneath it is still in the workflow (`SIGNOFF_CHILD_IN_WORKFLOW`, naming each one), since a parent's head proves its children's merged work: run each one to done, and sign off again. Park one with `park_child` only when a person asked for that move, or when the work is done, folded into another issue or no longer needed, and say in a comment which of those it is. Ask the person about a child that can reach neither `done` nor a park under that rule, and hold the acceptance until they answer. Nothing around the issue holds its acceptance, so an issue is accepted while its own root still runs, and you hold a parent's own sign-off until its last round (`skill://legion-architect`, section 4). `request_backward_move` with `issue`, `to` (the phase of an earlier step of the task: `implementing`, or `testing` where the task has a tester step) and `reason` sends such an issue back instead, starting that phase's role with the reason and counting a review round. The merge closes an issue; nothing after it is yours. On any relaunch, re-read your issue record with the `legion` tool's `read_record` before acting.
